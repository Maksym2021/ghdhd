Before you order

Which filters fit?

Any #02 cone filter, paper or cloth. A pack of 40 costs about €4 in most supermarkets and coffee shops.

Is it dishwasher safe?

Yes. The glaze is food safe and fired at 1280 °C, so it won’t craze or absorb coffee oils.

How fast will it arrive?

Orders paid before 13:00 leave Wrocław the same day. EU delivery takes 2–4 business days and you get a tracking link by email.

Can I return it?

Within 30 days of delivery, used or not, as long as it isn’t chipped. Send it back in the original box and we refund the full price.
