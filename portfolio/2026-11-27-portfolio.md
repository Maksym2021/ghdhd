```
Need a landing page that actually collects leads, instead of leaving them in an inbox?

I build fast, mobile-first landing pages in clean HTML, CSS and JavaScript and connect the form to where you work: Google Sheets, your CRM, Mailchimp, email or Telegram. With the Premium package, an n8n workflow also sends each lead a confirmation email and reminders automatically.

What you get:
• A single-goal page: clear offer, sections that answer objections, one strong call to action

• Leads delivered to Google Sheets, Telegram or your CRM

How it works: you send your offer, texts and images (or I draft the copy from your brief), I share the first version in 2–3 days, and we polish it together.

Please message me before ordering so we can confirm the scope.
```

**FAQ:**
- *Do you write the texts?* Yes, I can draft the copy from your brief. You approve it before the build.
- *Where will the page be hosted?* On your hosting, or I deploy it on Netlify or GitHub Pages and connect your domain.
- *Can leads go to my CRM?* Yes, if your CRM has an API or an n8n/Zapier integration (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho and many others). Ask me about yours before ordering.
- *What do you need from me?* Your offer, logo, images and where the leads should go. A site you like as a reference helps too.

**Requirements (вопросы покупателю):**
```
1. What do you sell, and to whom?
2. Texts and images, or a short brief so I can draft the copy.
3. Where should leads go: email, Google Sheets, Telegram or your CRM?
4. Your domain and hosting, if you have them.
5. Links to one or two sites you like.
```
